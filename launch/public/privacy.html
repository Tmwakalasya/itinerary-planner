City Tourist

Privacy

What the City Tourist app and this website collect, where it goes, and how to have it deleted.

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your trips stay on your iPhone. To plan them, the app asks a few services for places, events, weather and routes. This website keeps the email you sign up with and any survey answers you send, and nothing is sold to anyone.

The app

Your trips stay on your device

Trips, stops, saved places and settings are kept on your iPhone. We don't receive a copy. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location, only on the Today screen

If you allow it, the Today screen uses your location to work out how long it takes to reach your next stop and when to leave. That calculation goes to Apple Maps. Your location isn't sent to us or stored by the app. You can turn location off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Services the app asks, and what they get

  • Google Places: the city and area you're browsing, and the places on your trips, so it can show places, opening hours and photos. Covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Ticketmaster: your trip's city and dates, to find events on during it. Covered by Ticketmaster's privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo: the city's location and your trip's dates, for the forecast and sunset times.
  • Apple Maps: the stops (and, on Today, your location) between which it times a trip. Covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Like any app that goes online, these requests come from your device's internet address. The app doesn't send your name, your email or anything that identifies you.

Reminders

Leave-by reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They aren't sent from a server.

Shared trip links

When you share a trip, the plan is written into the link itself. It isn't stored on our servers. Anyone you send the link to can see the plan.

TestFlight

If you test the beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports, basic usage such as installs and sessions, and any feedback you send through TestFlight with us. Covered by Apple's privacy policy.

This website

Beta sign-up

When you sign up, we keep your email address and, if you gave it, your first name. We use them only to email you about the City Tourist beta. They're stored with Supabase, our database provider. A note that your address has signed up is also kept with Cloudflare, so you aren't emailed twice.

The beta survey

Survey answers are stored with Supabase, along with the city and app version the app adds to the link. They don't include your name. Your email is included only if you choose to leave it for a follow-up question.

Hosting and limits

The site runs on Cloudflare. To stop someone flooding the forms, your internet address is kept for up to an hour, with a count of how many times the forms were sent from it. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your internet address the way any website you visit does.

Your choices

  • To have your sign-up or survey answers deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email tmwakalasya@icloud.com.
  • To stop beta emails, reply and say so, and we'll take you off the list.
  • To remove everything the app keeps, delete the app.

Children

City Tourist isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date. If the change matters, we'll tell beta testers by email first.
