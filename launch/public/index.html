How a day comes together

Plan it, fix it, make it on time.

Three screens do most of the work. Scroll, and watch them go.

01 · Plan

Start with a day that works.

Pick a city and what you're into. City Tourist suggests a first day where every stop is open when you get there, with lunch at lunchtime and room to breathe between visits.

02 · Fix

One tap when the plan breaks.

A museum that closes before you'd arrive, a viewpoint after sunset, a hop you can't make in time: the day flags it. Fix this day swaps stops between the times you set until everything fits. Undo if you liked it better before.

03 · Go

Timed for how you get around.

Hops use Apple Maps transit times on that day's timetable, or driving if you have a car. Every leave-by time is one you can actually make, and Running late pushes the rest of the day back.

Also in the beta

The small things that make a day hold up.

What's on

Concerts, games and shows near your trip, from Ticketmaster. Add one and it goes into the day at its start time.

Weather and sunset

Every day shows the forecast and when the sun goes down. Outdoor stops after dark or in the rain get flagged.

Leave-by reminders

A notification when it's time to head to your next stop, timed from the one before or from where you're staying.

Any city

Search any city. Places and their opening hours come live from Google, and the plan adapts to the local time.
